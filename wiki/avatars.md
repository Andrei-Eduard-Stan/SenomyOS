# Avatar artwork

The native Rail avatar uses the shared skin registry's `persona.ring`,
`persona.portrait` and `persona.fallback`, resolved by `Config.RailStyle` and
`SenomyAvatar.qml`. Context-sized image requests bound decoding/render size.
The approved existing artwork remains the source; primary panel headers do not
reserve duplicate character art.

Use full Settings → Appearance for connected presentation preferences, including
backgrounds, reduced motion, hover expansion and Rail text size. Changing artwork
in the registry is source/configuration work: validate its path and dimensions
and review the relevant context before publishing watched changes.
