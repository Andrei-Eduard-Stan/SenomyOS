## Composition

Use a bespoke Cathedral Deck: shared header and route strip, central telemetry nave, secondary diagnostic zones, and persistent provenance footer. Preserve dense information while reducing equal-weight borders.

- Overview: paired machine-heart arcs and 300s history thread.
- CPU + GPU: package/frequency/thermal spine, load and thread topology.
- Memory: composition river and pressure thread.
- Storage: read/write ribbons, capacity and device topology.
- Network: mirrored duplex streams and link/route topology.
- Processes: full data plane with selection inspector.
- Benchmarks: warning gate, phase timeline, thermal/load thread, stop/cooldown/result states.

## Interaction and safety

Read-only telemetry remains visually separate from guarded controls. Preserve synchronized sampling, truthful unavailable/stale states, graceful process termination, exact PID/action confirmation, and benchmark power/thermal limits.

## Acceptance

Charts reveal relationships, scales, freshness, and unavailable states; text remains readable at the reference size; the surface is visually distinct from Control Centre and Insights.
