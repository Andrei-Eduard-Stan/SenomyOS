# Global surface contract

## Role

Make every Senomy-owned surface recognizably Luminous Reliquary without forcing identical layouts.

## Required structure

- Obsidian field, silver/ivory structural frame, sparse violet signal.
- Compact, Standard, or Large canonical frame with its exact safe inset.
- One dominant domain motif, never under content.
- Shared header, route, state, source, guarded-action, history, topology, ledger, and reading primitives where applicable.
- Loading, unavailable, empty, hover, focus, selected, disabled, warning, error, confirmation, running, success, and failure states.
- Standard and touch density; reduced-motion mode.

## Interaction

- One primary Eww surface at a time.
- Flyouts are transient and dismiss before or when opening a primary surface.
- Companion may coexist and never becomes a primary surface.
- Outside-dismiss remains a transparent functional layer. Any visual dim is a separate layer.
- Pointer, keyboard, and touch expose the same primary actions.

## Non-goals

- No broad cyan glow.
- No full character illustration in Control, Insights, or Performance.
- No arbitrary shell in Console.
- No decorative card around every row.
- No toolkit-forced imitation when a native token projection is safer.
